Privacy Policy

How the Neopath Technologies website handles your information. In short: we collect very little, and only what you choose to send us.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Who we are

This website is run by Neopath Technologies Pvt. Ltd. (“Neopath”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers this website only. Our products, PowerCampus and NeoEventize, are covered by the agreements and policies that apply to those services.

What we collect

Enquiries you send us. Our enquiry and contact forms do not send anything to our servers. When you press send, your own email app opens with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send that email. We then receive what you chose to include, such as your name, email address, phone number and project details.

Your theme choice. If you switch between light and dark mode, that choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It never leaves your device.

No cookies or analytics. This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Third-party services

Our fonts are served by Google Fonts. To show them, your browser connects to Google, which receives technical information such as your IP address and browser type. Google's handling of that information is described in its own privacy policy.

Like any website, the servers that host this site may keep standard technical logs (for example IP addresses and the pages requested) for security and reliability.

How we use your information

We use the details you send us only to reply to your enquiry, provide the services you ask about, and keep a record of our correspondence. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry emails for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records, and then delete them.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. You can also clear your theme choice at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
