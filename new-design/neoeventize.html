How It Works

From first ticket to last certificate.

A self-hosted ticketing and attendee operations platform that covers the whole event, not just the checkout.

  1. 01

    Set up your passes

    Single-day and multi-day all-access passes, VIP and Platinum tiers, early bird pricing and hidden promo codes.

  2. 02

    Sell and get paid directly

    Attendees pay by UPI, card or netbanking, and the money settles straight into your own account.

  3. 03

    Check in at the gate

    Every attendee gets a QR pass. Gate staff scan it with a phone camera in under 500ms.

  4. 04

    Certify and follow up

    Send personalised PDF certificates and a feedback survey once the event is over.
